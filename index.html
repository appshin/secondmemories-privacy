Second Memories Server ("the App") is operated by Mr.ShinJW ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

⚠ Important: All AI responses in this App are virtually generated content and do not represent actual statements, opinions, or intentions of the deceased. The AI conversations are for personal memorial purposes only.
